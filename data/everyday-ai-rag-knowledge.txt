University admissions use case
Title: Use case: university admissions review
Copy: Admissions teams can process large application volumes while keeping faculty or staff judgment focused on borderline files.

University admissions process flow
Step 1: Application Pack - Transcripts, SOPs, references, IDs, test scores, and supporting forms arrive from the portal.
Step 2: LlamaIndex Parsing - LlamaParse extracts text, tables, layouts, and structured fields from complex documents.
Step 3: Rubric Engine - Rules check academic fit, prerequisites, English evidence, completeness, and policy exceptions.
Step 4: Decision Queue - Strong admits and clear rejects are summarized; borderline files move to human review.
Step 5: Human Review - Reviewers see extracted evidence, rubric gaps, confidence notes, and suggested follow-up questions.
Step 6: System Update - Final decision, reviewer notes, and status are pushed back to the customer's CRM, SIS, spreadsheet, or workflow tool.

Example rubric signals
- Academic eligibility and prerequisite coverage
- Document completeness and authenticity checks
- Statement-of-purpose alignment with program criteria
- Reference strength and evidence of readiness
- Reviewer confidence score for borderline decisions

Borderline review
Reviewers see extracted facts, rubric gaps, confidence score, and recommended follow-up questions before making the final call.
